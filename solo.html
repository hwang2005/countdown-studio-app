How to Play Solo Practice

Letters Round: Press V (Vowel), C (Consonant), or R (Random 9). Once 9 tiles appear, click tiles or type letters on your keyboard. Press Enter to submit and view Dictionary Corner's longest words.

Numbers Round: Pick 0–4 large tiles. On the calculation board, click a number tile, click an operator (+ − × ÷), then click a second number tile to combine them into a new tile. Use Undo or Reset anytime before submitting.

Conundrum: Drag tiles to rearrange the 9-letter anagram or type your solution. Need help? Click Reveal 1 Letter (-3 pts).
