PUBLIC DOMAIN / PERMISSIVE WORD LIST LICENCE
============================================

The bundled offline word list (/data/words.txt) and conundrum dataset (/data/conundrums.json)
are derived from the public-domain ENABLE (Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon) and
the public-domain UKACD / dwyl english-words compilations, filtered to lowercase a-z words
of length 2 to 9 letters suitable for Countdown letters rounds and 9-letter conundrums.
